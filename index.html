About Arpan

More Than Just a Meal.

Ranchi, Arpan Restaurant & Catering was built around a simple idea: good food, shared in good company, is worth doing properly. Our kitchen moves between North Indian, Mughlai, Chinese and South Indian cooking, so there's something that fits whoever you've brought along — a first date, a family dinner, or a table of twelve celebrating something.

The dining room is set up for comfort as much as looks — proper spacing between tables, seating that works for kids and grandparents alike, and a pace that doesn't rush you. Beyond the restaurant, our catering team carries that same care to birthdays, get-togethers and larger celebrations, wherever you're hosting them.
